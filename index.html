Equations

y = pitch in semitones · x = beats
How it works
  • Height = pitch. Each step up on the y axis is one semitone above the root note. 12 is an octave up.
  • Left to right = time. Each grid column is one beat. Playback starts at the far left and loops.
  • Tone glides smoothly along the curve. 808 hits on a beat grid and follows the curve with a deep, punchy bass.
  • Limit a curve with braces: sin(x)*5 {2 < x < 6}. Outside the range is silence.
  • Try floor(x), mod(x, 4), abs(x - 8), sqrt(x), 2^x.
